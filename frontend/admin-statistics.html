Cyclic Recruitment Architecture

The cyclic model divides college placements into 4 predictable quarters to eliminate recruitment congestion and optimize student preparation timelines.

Q1 (Jan–Mar): High-conversion Spring Internships & Startup SDE roles.
Q2 (Apr–Jun): Consulting, Cloud Platforms & Pre-placement interviews.
Q3 (Jul–Sep): Autumn Mega Campus Season & Super Dream Day-1 Giants.
Q4 (Oct–Dec): Winter Dream Drives, Core Engineering & Fintech low-latency systems.
